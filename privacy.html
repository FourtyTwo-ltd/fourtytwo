Privacy Policy

Last updated December 2026

This page is a starter template describing our data practices in plain terms. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be treated as final, binding legal copy — have qualified counsel review and adapt it before relying on it.

What We Collect

When you use this site, we collect:

  • Standard analytics data — page views, device category, approximate geographic location, and traffic source, via our analytics provider.
  • Interaction events — clicks on ticket, table-booking, phone, and email links, so we can understand what people are looking for.
  • Information you submit directly — such as an email address entered into the newsletter form.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (not third-party cookies) to remember whether background audio should be playing and where you left off, so it isn't reset every time you move between pages. Our analytics provider, once configured, may set its own cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions.

How We Use It

We use this data to understand how people use the site, to improve the events, products, and content we put out, and to respond to inquiries sent to us directly. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Your Rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, by contacting contact@fourtytwo.com. You can also clear locally stored data at any time from your browser's settings.
