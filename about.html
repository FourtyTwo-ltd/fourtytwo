About 42

A cultural platform built for the long term

Who We Are

42 is a culture and experiences company owned by WayKON Holdings. We are building a long-term cultural platform, not a one-dimensional event business.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria, 42 develops recognizable properties, experiences, products, and cultural projects under one master brand with a globally credible identity.

What We Do

Experiences

Selective, high-quality cultural events that create lasting impact.

Objects

Limited physical products designed with intention and precision.

Calendar

Curated discovery platform for cultural moments in Lagos.

Archive

The permanent historical record of 42 and its projects.
