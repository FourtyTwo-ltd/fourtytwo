42 is a culture and experiences company owned by WayKON Holdings. We are building a long-term cultural platform, not a one-dimensional event business — developing recognizable properties, experiences, products, and cultural projects under one master brand with a globally credible identity.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria, our work spans curated experiences, limited physical objects, a discovery calendar for cultural moments, and a permanent archive — each selective, intentional, and built for long-term brand value over frequency or hype.

We are not a generic party promotion company, a conventional nightclub promoter, a cheap nightlife brand, or an influencer-driven label. Every project we release is measured against financial viability, operational control, and the atmosphere it creates — culture built with intention, not chased for attention.
